# Fictional catalogue exercise

This note was created solely to demonstrate local byte-integrity checking.

Question: What is the sum of the integers from 1 to 10?

Pair the first and last terms: 1 + 10 = 11, 2 + 9 = 11, 3 + 8 = 11,
4 + 7 = 11 and 5 + 6 = 11. Five pairs give 5 × 11 = 55.

Scope: an elementary worked example. It is not a claim of mathematical novelty,
a benchmark of agent performance or an independently reviewed research finding.
